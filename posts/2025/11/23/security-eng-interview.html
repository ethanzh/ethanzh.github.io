Interviewing Security Software Engineers

2025-11-23

- Scattered thoughts on how to approach hiring security engineer #2+ at a startup
Tags: security-engineering, hiring

I played the role as founding/solo security engineer at Lumos for around 3 and a half years before beginning to grow the team. During this time I owned various functions such as IT, Trust & Compliance, and Security Engineering.

IT became it's own team, but I continue to own T&C and SecEng. As I approached hiring the Second security engineer I drew up a lofty laundry list of requirements: - 3+ yoe - experience implementing security solutions for apps and systems e.g. auth flows, authz frameworks, secrets management - strong backend dev skills (python or ts is a +) - threat modeling, red teaming, pentesting - cloud experience (aws preferred) - infrastructure as code experience - experience at growth stage start ups is a plus - iga and iam familiarity is a plus - product thinking - developer experience

This did not include a few things I originally wanted to include, but decided to take out: - experience conducting SOC 2 / ISO 27001 audits - experience with IT security e.g. Okta

The rationale here was that we were separately hiring for an IT Engineer who would be handling these responsibilities internally.
